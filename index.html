Lantern Hunt

A decentralized, people-powered campaign to protect native habitats and eradicate invasive species.

Our first target is the spotted lanternfly. Lantern Hunt helps everyday people find, remove, and document it, one verified removal at a time.

Why it matters

Invasive species can crowd out native plants and wildlife and disrupt the habitats they depend on. Lantern Hunt starts from a simple idea: many people noticing and acting in their own neighborhoods can add up.

The spotted lanternfly is our first focus. Over time, we hope to expand the campaign to other invasive species.

What Lantern Hunt is

Lantern Hunt is an iPhone app that helps you:

Identify

Learn to recognize spotted lanternflies and their egg masses.

Remove & document

Photograph and record the lanternflies and egg masses you remove.

See the effort

View verified removal activity from the community on a shared map.

To keep people coming back, the app also includes points, a leaderboard, streaks, and collectible companions. See how it works →

An independent project. Lantern Hunt is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency or environmental organization. For official guidance on invasive species in your area, consult your state or local agricultural or natural resources authorities.
