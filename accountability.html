Accountability

How a removal becomes a number on this site.

Every figure Lantern Hunt publishes comes from submissions a person has reviewed. This page explains what a submission contains, how it is reviewed, how the statistics are calculated, and how participants’ locations and privacy are protected.

What a submission contains Review Statistics Location data Privacy

The submission

What a participant sends.

Each removal is submitted from the participant’s phone as a single record.

Type
Adult spotted lanternfly or egg mass
Quantity
How many the participant removed
Photos
A before and an after photograph, with the time each was taken
Location
Latitude and longitude from the phone, with its GPS accuracy estimate, when the phone provides them
Review
Submission time, review status, review date, and any rejection reason

Review

A person reviews every submission.

The reviewer sees the full submission, including both photographs, the type and quantity entered, and the full-precision location. Nothing counts until it has been approved.

Submitted

The participant sends the removal

Type, quantity, two photos, and the location, if available, arrive together. Nothing is visible to other users yet.

In review

A reviewer examines it

The reviewer looks at the photographs and the details entered, then approves or rejects the submission.

Approved

Counts as verified

Points are confirmed, and the removal appears on the in-app community map at reduced precision.

Rejected

Does not count

No points, and it never appears on the map or in any statistic.

Review checks the photographs and details of each submission. It is not a laboratory identification or an on-site inspection.

Statistics

How the figures are calculated.

The figures on this site are calculated from an export of the app’s submission records. They are updated by hand, and each one shows the date it was calculated.

What counts
Approved submissions only. Pending and rejected submissions are left out.
Start date
Submissions from the public launch on September 29, 2026 onward. Earlier submissions were pre-launch testing.
Verified kills
The total quantity on approved adult submissions
Egg masses
Counted separately and never added to kills, because one egg mass holds 30 to 50 eggs rather than one insect
Geolocated submissions
Approved submissions, adult or egg mass, that include a latitude and longitude
Duplicates
Each submission is counted once
Rounding
Headline figures are rounded down, so 2,067 appears as 2,000+

Location data

Useful for mapping, careful about homes.

A removal often happens at someone’s home. Exact locations are seen only by the reviewer. Everyone else sees them at reduced precision or grouped by county.

Full precision
Visible only to the reviewer, never to other users
In-app community map
Approved removals only, shown at reduced precision of about 10 meters. That is still precise enough to suggest a street or property.
Pending and rejected
Not visible to other users
Website map
Each location is assigned to a state and county, and the coordinates are then discarded. No coordinates are published on this site.
Small numbers
An area shows an exact count only when at least three people contributed and it has at least ten kills. Otherwise it shows a range, such as “fewer than 10.”
No location
Approved removals submitted without a location count in the totals but do not appear on the map

Privacy

What we collect and what we don’t.

Accounts
No name or email address required
Photo metadata
Uploaded images are created fresh, without the original files’ embedded GPS tags
Tracking
No third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software in the app
Selling data
We do not sell personal information
