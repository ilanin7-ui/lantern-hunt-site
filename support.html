Common questions

Why hasn't my removal counted yet?

Removals are reviewed before they are verified. Points and map activity update after a removal is verified.

Where can I find official guidance on spotted lanternflies?

Your state or local agricultural or natural resources authorities are the best source of official guidance for your area. Lantern Hunt is an independent project and does not speak for any agency.
